OmniTool privacy

Sensor readings, calibration and measurement history are stored on your device. OmniTool has no account or cloud measurement service. Data leaves the app only when you choose a CSV or JSON export destination.

The microphone is requested only after you start the estimated sound meter. Processing is local and the temporary audio file is deleted when the meter stops; it is not uploaded or retained by OmniTool.

Google Mobile Ads may process device identifiers, advertising data, product interaction, coarse location, performance and crash data according to your consent choices and Google's policies. Advertising privacy choices are available in Settings.

Deleting recordings or uninstalling the app removes its local history. Files you exported are controlled by the destination you selected.
