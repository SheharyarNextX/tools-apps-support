OmniTool support

Sensor unavailable

Not every phone contains every sensor. Remove magnetic cases, keep the device still, and reopen the app before trying again.

Sound meter unavailable

Tap Start and allow microphone access. OmniTool does not retain the temporary audio. Simulator and emulator microphones may not provide meaningful levels.

Ruler accuracy

Use the display calibration controls to match 85.60 mm to a standard bank-card width before measuring.

Advertising choices

Open Settings and select Advertising privacy choices. Rewarded viewing is optional and core tools remain free.

OmniTool is not a certified sound meter, surveying instrument, medical device, or safety instrument.
