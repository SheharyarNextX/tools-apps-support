Your loan log, explained

Privacy

Updated 20 September 2026.

Summary

ReturnPing is a personal log of physical items lent to other people. It has no account system or developer-operated cloud-sync service. Every loan feature is free. Your loan records are not supplied to advertising services as targeting information.

Information you enter

The app stores item labels, borrower text, notes, optional due dates, follow-up priorities and saved creation/return dates in local app storage. Borrower names and notes may contain personal information. Enter only details you need; avoid passwords, financial credentials and other sensitive information. No encryption guarantee is made for local records.

ReturnPing does not upload these loan records to a Software House server or access your contact book. Your operating system may include local app data in backups or device transfers according to its settings and behaviour. Those services have their own privacy controls.

Export, deletion and retention

Export all loans as CSV creates a file containing active and returned records, then opens the device share interface. You choose its destination. The selected app or service handles the exported copy under its own terms. A temporary export copy can remain in the app cache until the system clears it or the app is removed. There is no CSV import, JSON restore or automatic cloud backup feature in ReturnPing.

Marking an item returned keeps its record in History. To remove a record from the working loan list, choose Delete record and confirm, then allow saving to finish. An older data-format copy preserved during an app migration can remain in local app storage even after a record is removed from the working list. Clearing app storage or deleting the app removes the app's local working and preserved migration data. Removing a record does not remove copies you exported, emailed or placed in a device backup. Delete those separately using the destination's controls.

Advertising and your choices

ReturnPing includes Google Mobile Ads and Google's User Messaging Platform. Advertising remains disabled in pre-release preparation. In an advertising-enabled release, the app checks consent before initializing ads or requesting an advertisement. On iOS, this app additionally requires granted App Tracking Transparency permission before advertising requests. Denial, unavailable consent or ad-loading errors leave every loan feature usable.

When advertising services are used, Google may process your IP address and approximate location inferred from it, device or other identifiers, ad and app interaction information, and diagnostic/performance information for advertising, analytics, security and fraud prevention. Requesting non-personalized ads does not mean these services collect no data. ReturnPing does not attach item, borrower, note, search or CSV contents to ad requests. See Google's Privacy Policy and Google's advertising privacy controls.

Open Plan, then Advertising privacy choices to manage an available Google consent form. Opening that control turns off the app's current ad readiness; closing it alone does not restart ads. A form may be unavailable or not required in your region. You can also manage app tracking permission on iOS and advertising identifiers through your device settings. Google's retention and deletion practices are governed by its policies and controls; deleting ReturnPing does not itself request deletion of Google's records.

The optional rewarded advertisement changes only a temporary amber highlight on its own card. Adding, editing, searching, due-date organization, returned history and CSV export never require an advertisement or payment.

Permissions and limits

ReturnPing does not read contacts, use the camera or microphone, obtain GPS location, send borrower messages or schedule automatic notifications. Dates and priorities organize the on-screen list only. Sharing is initiated by you. Network access supports consent, advertising and links you choose to open, not a loan-sync service.

Contact and changes

For privacy questions, email tools@softwarehouse.au. Include the app version and your question, but avoid sending borrower details or a full export unless necessary. This notice will be updated if the app's data practices change.
