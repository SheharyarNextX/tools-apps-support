Support

Help with your free, personal item-loan log.

Quick start

  1. Open Add and enter the physical item and borrower. Add an optional note or due date in YYYY-MM-DD format.
  2. Save, then find the record on Loans. Search by item, person or note, or choose Edit loan to change it.
  3. Choose Mark returned and confirm when the item comes back. The saved record moves to History.
  4. Reopen a returned loan from History if needed. Delete record removes it from the working loan list after confirmation and successful saving; see Privacy for retained migration, export and backup copies.

Due dates and follow-up

Valid due dates and the optional Follow up soon priority help organize the list and Plan counts. No automatic notification, borrower message, payment, debt calculation or guarantee of return is provided. This is a log of physical items, not a money-lending service.

Export your records

Choose Export all loans as CSV from Plan. The file includes active and returned records. Pick a destination in the device share interface. There is no in-app CSV import or restore, so an export is a portable record, not a one-tap app backup. Files you share may include borrower details; choose the destination carefully.

Saving problems

  • If saved loans cannot be read, the app leaves the stored data untouched and offers Retry loading. Do not clear app storage or uninstall while investigating data you need.
  • If Changes are not saved yet appears, keep the app open and use Retry save. Wait for the saving indicator to finish.
  • If sharing fails, your loan list remains unchanged. Check that a compatible Files or sharing destination is available, then try again.
  • If search appears empty, clear the search text and check Loans versus History.

Free features and advertising privacy

All loan features remain free, including history and CSV export. There are no purchases, subscriptions, trials or paywalls. Advertising is disabled in pre-release preparation. In an advertising-enabled release, consent and iOS tracking permission are checked before ad requests. Use Plan → Advertising privacy choices to manage the available consent form, or your device settings to manage tracking. Unavailable ads or declined permission never block loan features.

The optional Watch ad for optional highlight action can tint only its own card amber for 30 minutes. It does not change loan records or unlock a paid feature. See the privacy notice for details.
