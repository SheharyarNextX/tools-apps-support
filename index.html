Using the apps

No account or sign-in is required. Records and optional photos are stored on the device unless the user chooses to export or share them through another app. All app features are included without purchases or usage limits.

Common help

If a saved record is unavailable, confirm the app has not been removed and the device storage has not been cleared. Camera access is optional and is used only when the user chooses to attach a site or job photo. PDF and share actions use the device share interface.
