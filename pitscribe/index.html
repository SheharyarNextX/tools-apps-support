Offline BBQ cook log

Keep every meat and every moment on the same timeline.

PitScribe helps you run simultaneous meats with independent timers, record the cook as it happens and keep a searchable history for next time.

Built for the active cook

Independent meat timers

Track several meats in one cook without forcing them onto the same schedule.

Quick cook events

Log a user-entered temperature, spritz, wrap, refuel or rest with its place on the timeline.

Smoker profiles

Save the equipment and setup details you want to reuse on a later cook.

History and comparison

Search saved cooks, review what happened and compare earlier records.

Portable records

Export CSV, JSON or readable text through your device's share options.

No account required

Cook records stay on your device. There is no PitScribe cloud account to create.

Food-safety note: PitScribe records values entered by you. It does not measure temperature, determine doneness or provide a food-safety verdict. Use a reliable thermometer and current guidance from an appropriate food-safety authority.

Free, supported by advertising

Every core feature is free. PitScribe has no subscriptions, in-app purchases, trials, paywalls or paid-only tools. Advertising is intended to stay away from critical timer and logging actions.
